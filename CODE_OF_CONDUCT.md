# 社区行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 中文

参与本组织即表示你同意本准则，适用于仓库、讨论区、Issue、Pull Request 与其他社区空间。

**我们期待的行为**

- 就事论事：批评内容，不针对人。
- 尊重署名：引用与改编注明来源。
- 保护隐私：不在公开空间发布他人的姓名、邮箱、学号、聊天记录。
- 遵守课程与学校规定：不上传保密材料或他人未授权的资料。
- 接受审核：维护者可以要求修改或移除违规内容。

**不可接受的行为**

- 人身攻击、骚扰、歧视、威胁。
- 冒充他人身份或伪造贡献记录。
- 上传考试答案、未公开试题、教师未授权材料或科研保密资料。
- 未经同意公开他人个人信息。
- 恶意提交、刷量、破坏仓库或绕过审核。
- 利用本组织进行商业推广而未事先说明。

**处理**

视情节采取：提醒、要求更正、移除内容、限制发言或贡献、移出组织。
严重情况可直接移出。处理决定由维护者作出并记录；当事人可提出申诉，
由其他维护者复核。

**举报**

通过 [community issues](https://github.com/westlake-drawer/community/issues) 或私下联系维护者。
涉及个人隐私或安全的内容请勿公开发布。举报会按隐私政策处理。

## English

By participating you agree to this code, which applies to repositories, discussions,
issues, pull requests, and other community spaces.

**Expected**: critique content, not people; attribute sources; protect privacy (never
post others' names, emails, student IDs, or chat logs); follow course and university
rules; accept moderation.

**Unacceptable**: personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats; impersonation
or falsified contribution records; uploading exam answers, unreleased papers,
unauthorised instructor material, or confidential research; publishing personal data
without consent; malicious commits, spam, or circumventing review; undisclosed
commercial promotion.

**Enforcement**: a warning, a correction request, content removal, restricted posting
or contribution rights, or removal from the organization. Serious cases may be removed
immediately. Maintainers decide and record the outcome; the person may appeal to a
different maintainer.

**Reporting**: through [community issues](https://github.com/westlake-drawer/community/issues)
or privately to a maintainer. Do not post private or security-sensitive details in
public; reports are handled under the privacy policy.
