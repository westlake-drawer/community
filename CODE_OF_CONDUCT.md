# Code of Conduct

By participating you agree to this code, which applies to repositories, discussions,
issues, pull requests, and other community spaces.

## Expected

- Critique content, not people.
- Attribute sources when quoting or adapting.
- Protect privacy: never post another person's name, email, student ID, or chat logs.
- Follow course and university rules; do not upload confidential or unauthorised material.
- Accept moderation: maintainers may ask for changes or remove content.

## Unacceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats.
- Impersonation or falsified contribution records.
- Uploading exam answers, unreleased papers, unauthorised instructor material, or
  confidential research.
- Publishing personal data without consent.
- Malicious commits, spam, or circumventing review.
- Undisclosed commercial promotion.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity: a warning, a correction request, content removal, restricted
posting or contribution rights, or removal from the organization. Serious cases may be
removed immediately. Maintainers decide and record the outcome; the person may appeal
to a different maintainer.

## Reporting

Through [community issues](https://github.com/westlake-drawer/community/issues) or
privately to a maintainer. Do not post private or security-sensitive details in
public; reports are handled under the
[privacy policy](https://westlake-drawer.github.io/legal/privacy).
